My journey so far

Nine lives, one step at a time.

  1. 2024 — now

    Fishwell Project · Orient Software (Neurond)

    Building AI that monitors fish for fish farmers in Norway. My job: teach a computer to watch fish all day without getting bored, which is more than I can say for any cat. Pretty sure I've seen more fish than people this year.

  2. 2022

    AI Engineer @ Orient Software

    Officially became an AI Engineer: I explain things to computers until they learn. It turns out models are much better listeners than cats. They still ignore me sometimes, but at least they don't knock my coffee off the desk.

  3. 2021

    First job: Android Intern

    Signed up "just for fun." Accidentally built a whole app. Fun got slightly out of hand, and that's how you find out you actually enjoy this job.

  4. 2017 — 2022

    University · Faculty of IT

    Studied IT, practiced my English at the Hugo English Club, and joined the University's Youth Union. Also learned the most important engineering lesson: "it works on my machine" is not a valid defense.
